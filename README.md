## About me

I'm a backend engineer based in Portugal. I joined the Crypto team at [Uphold](https://uphold.com) in 2020 and I'm now a Lead Crypto Engineer there.

My work combines hands-on backend development with technical leadership. Most of it involves blockchain integrations, system design and production operations, and I work with other engineering teams to deliver cross-team initiatives.

Things I care about at work:

- Finding ways to take manual steps out of routine operational work.
- Keeping tech debt in check before it piles up.
- Making day-to-day development smoother for the team.
- Trying out AI-assisted development with my team and sharing what works.
